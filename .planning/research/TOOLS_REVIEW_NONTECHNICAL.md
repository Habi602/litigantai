## PART 1 — BUNDLING

### BunTool
- **Website:** buntool.co.uk
- **Founded:** March 2026
- **Price:** Free
- **What it does:** Creates indexed, bookmarked, paginated PDFs from multiple documents. All processing happens locally in the browser — no account, no upload to a server.
- **Built by:** A solicitor at Bird & Bird.
- **Why it matters:** Direct competitor. Just launched. Test immediately and document every feature it has and every gap you find.
- **Test questions:** Can it handle 100+ documents? Does it OCR scanned PDFs? Can you reorder documents after upload? Does the hyperlinked index work in court PDF viewers?

---

### Zylpha
- **Website:** zylpha.com
- **Founded:** 2012
- **Price:** Free up to 350 pages / 30MB; £20/month paid tier
- **What it does:** Drag-and-drop bundling with automatic page numbering and a hyperlinked table of contents. UK court compliant.
- **Why it matters:** Simple, established, free tier. Benchmark for what "good enough for free" looks like.
- **Test questions:** What happens when you exceed 350 pages? How does the paid tier differ? How long does a 50-document bundle take to generate?

---

### Bundledocs
- **Website:** bundledocs.com
- **Founded:** 2012
- **Price:** Subscription (paid — request a trial)
- **What it does:** Cloud-based. Fully automated indexing, sectioning, and pagination. Re-bundles instantly when you make changes. Integrates with law firm software (Clio, iManage). Used in 30+ countries.
- **Why it matters:** The professional standard for smaller firms. Has cross-document referencing built in.
- **Test questions:** How does re-bundling work when one document changes? What does cross-referencing look like in practice? How is it priced per user vs per bundle?

---

### TrialView
- **Website:** trialview.com
- **Founded:** 2015
- **Price:** Enterprise (contact for pricing)
- **What it does:** Converts Excel, Word, PDF, and email into court-compliant bundles. AI OCR, smart pagination, cross-referencing, and timeline creation. De facto standard for complex UK litigation.
- **Why it matters:** This is what top-tier litigation teams use. Sets the ceiling for what the market expects. Request a demo.
- **Test questions:** What does the AI cross-referencing actually do — automatic or manual? How does timeline creation work? What's the per-matter cost?

## PART 2 — STITCHING / CROSS-REFERENCING

### Clearbrief
- **Website:** clearbrief.com
- **Founded:** 2019
- **Price:** Enterprise (AmLaw 200 clients)
- **What it does:** AI brief editor that automatically hyperlinks citations in a legal brief to the source documents. Detects hallucinated citations. Generates a table of authorities.
- **Why it matters:** Closest existing product to what NoahLaw's stitching feature would do. Used by major US law firms.
- **Test questions:** Request a demo. Does it work on UK-style bundles or only US briefs? Does it link backwards — from source document to the brief that cites it?

---

### TypeLaw
- **Website:** typelaw.com
- **Founded:** 2020
- **Price:** Not publicly listed
- **What it does:** Auto-OCRs PDFs, builds a hyperlinked index, and automatically links citations in briefs to the source authority and record.
- **Why it matters:** Strong citation-to-document linking. Worth comparing directly to Clearbrief.
- **Test questions:** Request access. How does it handle UK case citations vs US? What's the workflow — upload bundle first, then write brief, or the reverse?

## PART 3 — LEGAL MARKETPLACES

### Lawhive
- **Website:** lawhive.co.uk
- **Founded:** 2019
- **Price:** Variable (fixed + hourly fees)
- **What it does:** Connects UK clients with SRA-regulated solicitors. Has a fixed-fee and hourly model. Built an AI lawyer called "Lawrence."
- **Why it matters:** Raised $40M Series A in December 2024. Biggest UK competitor. Study how they onboard clients, how they present cases to lawyers, and what "Lawrence" actually does.
- **Test questions:** Go through the full client journey — post a legal problem and see what happens. How does Lawrence compare to NoahLaw's AI? How are specialists matched? What does pricing look like end to end?

---

### LiPs AI
- **Website:** lipsai.co.uk
- **Founded:** 2020s
- **Price:** £299 per document
- **What it does:** Document automation specifically for civil court claims and defences in England & Wales. Claims to be 95% cheaper than solicitors.
- **Why it matters:** Direct LIP-focused competitor. Same audience as NoahLaw. But per-document pricing vs NoahLaw's platform approach.
- **Test questions:** What does the output document look like? How much human input is required? Is it truly automated or is there a lawyer reviewing it behind the scenes?

---

### Courtroom5
- **Website:** courtroom5.com
- **Founded:** 2015
- **Price:** Subscription
- **What it does:** Platform for self-represented litigants. Document templates, case law search, evidence management, community forum. 10,000+ users. US-focused.
- **Why it matters:** Most directly comparable to NoahLaw's case management features. Study their UX and what 10,000 real LIPs actually use.
- **Test questions:** Sign up for a trial. What does their evidence management look like? How do they handle the timeline? What's missing that NoahLaw has? What do they have that NoahLaw lacks?

---

### myBarrister
- **Website:** mybarrister.co.uk
- **Founded:** 2020s
- **Price:** Free to search; barrister fees vary
- **What it does:** Connects the public directly to barristers for 70+ areas of law. Claims 30-50% cheaper than going via a solicitor.
- **Why it matters:** Leading UK direct-access barrister platform. Relevant for how NoahLaw's marketplace should work — should specialists include barristers, not just solicitors?
- **Test questions:** Browse the directory. How are barristers presented — fixed fees, hourly, both? Is there any matching logic or is it pure search? How does a case get scoped for a barrister?
